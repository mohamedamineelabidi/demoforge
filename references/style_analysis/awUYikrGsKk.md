## Style verdict

1. Kinetic-typography SaaS launch: big centred words with motion blur carry the story, screenshots on tilted planes provide proof (sheet_1, 3, 5).
2. Two-tone world: white with a blue bottom glow for calm beats, saturated/navy blue radial fields for emphasis, one black tagline end card.
3. Problem beat greys out competitor-style windows in the margins; solution beat lights the dashboard with a glow on saturated blue.
4. Slow detected cut rate (11 shots in 95 s) but dense internal text beats; heavy blur transitions make it feel faster than the counts suggest.
5. Useful lessons: bracket framing device, typed caret opener and closer, keyword colour swap inside a sentence, glowing dashboard on a tilted plane; the 95 s length and repeated word-only beats are too slow for a 30 s demo.
