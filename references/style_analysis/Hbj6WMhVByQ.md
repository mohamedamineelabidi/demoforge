## Style verdict

1. Brand launch film, not a UI demo: colour blocks, mosaics and huge kinetic type cut on the beat (sheet_1-4).
2. Bold hard-edged palette (blue #2A3DFF / black / off-white / yellow) with no gradients except one purple field.
3. Product UI appears only twice: laptop screenshot (frames 558-572) and translucent dashboard floating over desk footage (frames 608-760).
4. Pacing is far faster than demo norms: 26 cuts in 72 s, flashes as short as 14 frames.
5. Useful lessons: pill captions with white text on black, footage inside rounded-corner cards; avoid its rapid cut rhythm for 30 s demos.
