## Style attributes

- **layout:** UI-explainer: real product UI elements (buttons, menus, dialogs, panels) cut out and enlarged in the centre of a clean field, alternating with typed headlines; full editor only in short flashes
- **background:** white #FFFFFF and very pale blue-pink gradients #EAF1FF -> #F6E9F4; one saturated Google blue #1A73E8 field and one blue gradient for emphasis
- **typography:** Google Sans / Product Sans, regular weight, sentence case, dark grey #3C4043 with blue #1A73E8 keyword accents; headlines around 1/8 frame height; typing caret used as a device
- **palette_hex:** #FFFFFF, #EAF1FF, #1A73E8, #4285F4, #3C4043, #34A853, #FBBC04, #EA4335, #A142F4, #0F5F5F
- **camera_motion:** none on footage; zoom-through on keywords into dialogs, card tilts in 3D, slow scale on hero pills
- **transitions:** hard cuts (20) plus zoom-through from typed word into UI, blur dissolve; a rapid six-beat run at 30-frame intervals (frames 1926-2155)
- **ui_highlight:** isolate and enlarge one control (New button, voice row, Play button) with a soft shadow or blue outline ring; oversized hand cursor with click; rest of the UI hidden
- **text_animation:** typewriter with blinking caret, keyword colour swap grey->blue, keyword scales up and becomes the next scene, generated text streams in at giant size
- **pacing_frames:** 21 shots avg 124 frames, min 14, max 365; slow typed headline beats alternate with fast 30-frame UI flashes
- **cursor_or_hand:** yes, oversized white hand pointer with click animation on every interaction
- **real_ui_vs_motion:** real Google Vids UI fragments re-composed as motion design; stock footage and presenter webcam inside slides; no raw screen recording
- **audio:** audio stream present (voiceover-style pacing, music)
- **three_beat:** yes: intent typed as prompt (frames 0-404) -> generation and editing steps (frames 404-2155) -> result played back and use-case list + logo (frames 2155-2602)

## Style verdict

1. The clearest "isolate one control" reference: each step shows a single enlarged UI element (pill, menu row, dialog, Play button) on a white field with an oversized hand cursor (sheet_1-5).
2. Typed headline with blinking caret and blue keyword introduces every step; the keyword itself zooms into the UI it names.
3. Pastel white-blue palette with Google brand accents; UI always sits on white or a light gradient, full editor appears only for a few 30-frame flashes.
4. Pacing mixes 90-400-frame explanatory holds with a rapid six-beat run of 30-frame cuts; total 21 shots in 87 s.
5. Useful lessons for 30 s demos: prompt-pill hero card, chips flying into the prompt, generated text streaming at large size, blue outline ring on the clicked button; the typed-caret device is cheap to reproduce in HTML overlays.
