## Style verdict

1. Fast beat-cut launch teaser: 22 shots in 42 s, avg 57 frames, alternating blue/off-white/dark cards (sheet_1-4).
2. Product UI is shown on 3D tablet/laptop mockups and as huge isolated controls slammed in with motion blur (frames 801-854).
3. Best reusable beat: table -> 'Generate Feedback' button fills frame -> zoomed result text (frames 749-950).
4. One blue keyword per caption plus an italic serif accent word is the typographic signature.
5. No cursor; action is implied by the control zoom, not by pointer movement.
