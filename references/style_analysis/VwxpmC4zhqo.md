- **background:** mostly white to pale blue-lavender gradient #F4F6FB -> #DDE3F5, with one dark navy #0B1022 3D sequence and one black field with blue/purple liquid light streaks
- **typography:** geometric sans (Gilroy/Montserrat-like), medium weight, sentence case, dark navy #1B2340 on light fields, white on dark; single giant words ("Intuitive", "Converse Bank") at roughly 1/6 frame height

- **pacing_frames:** 8 shots avg 165 frames, min 21, max 293; calm compared with brand films, roughly one idea every 4-5 s
- **cursor_or_hand:** none

- **audio:** audio stream present (opus); edits land on soft beats

## Style verdict

1. Classic app-launch structure: cold open on the problem, phone-mockup feature beats, App Store CTA and logo end card (sheet_1-3).
2. Light airy palette (white to pale blue gradient, navy text, blue accent) with two contrasting dark 3D sequences for energy.
3. UI is real but composited: screenshots inside phone mockups, feature rows extracted as white cards with shadows, toggles animated to show the "customisable" claim.
4. Pacing is moderate: 8 shots in 44 s, most holds 100-300 frames, one 21-frame flash; readable for a 30 s demo.
5. Useful lessons: isolate a UI row as a floating card to explain one feature, use a single giant word as a breather beat, end on a store-card CTA; avoid the heavy 3D phone arrays which need a 3D pipeline.
